RealMusic AI — Privacy Policy

Effective: November 15, 2026 · Anni Yang / Venturebnb LLC · Contact: info@annizon.com

RealMusic AI turns lyrics you write and a style you describe into a finished, sung song. This policy says exactly what is stored, what is shared, with whom, and when.

YOUR ACCOUNT

RealMusic AI uses an email sign-in link (no password), provided by Supabase, our database and sign-in provider. Your account holds your email address and your timezone. Your songs, generation jobs and credit balance belong to this account, which is why signing in is required to create.

WHAT WE STORE

To run the service we store: your style descriptions and the lyrics you submit (exactly as you wrote them — they are never silently rewritten); your pronunciation-check picks for Chinese lyrics; your generation jobs and their outcomes; the finished songs, kept in private storage that only your account can open; your credit ledger — an append-only record of grants, purchases, holds, captures and releases that is the record of your balance; and the push-notification token for your device, so we can tell you when a song is ready.

GENERATION PROVIDERS

Songs are generated on our server by third-party AI music providers (currently MiniMax models served through Fal and Replicate). When you tap generate, our server sends your style description and lyrics to the provider, receives the audio, and stores it in your private library. Your account identity and email are never sent to a generation provider. Provider audio links expire, so the copy in your library is the one we keep for you.

PAYMENTS

Credit packs and subscriptions are sold through the App Store or Google Play and processed by RevenueCat, our purchase provider. We never see or store your card. The store tells us what was bought, and your credit ledger records the credits it granted. Subscriptions renew and are cancelled in the store's own subscription settings; cancelling never removes credits already granted, and credits never expire.

YOUR DATA IS NEVER SOLD

There is no sale or sharing of your data with third parties for their own purposes. Your data goes only to the providers that run the app — Supabase (database, sign-in, song storage), the generation providers above (your lyrics and style text only), RevenueCat and the app stores (purchases) — and only what each needs to do its work. There is no advertising and no analytics SDK.

YOUR SONGS ARE YOURS

Songs in your library are visible only to your account. Downloads are never capped and are never removed by us. Deleting a song (in the player) removes its audio file and record. Deleting your account (Settings → Delete my account) removes everything — songs, jobs, ledger and profile — right away; if you no longer have the app, email info@annizon.com from your account email and we will delete it by hand and confirm.

QUESTIONS

Contact info@annizon.com. A human answers within 2 business days.
