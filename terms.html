RealMusic AI — Terms of Use

Effective: November 15, 2026 · Anni Yang / Venturebnb LLC · Contact: info@annizon.com

ORIGINAL SONGS ONLY — THE CONTRACT

RealMusic AI creates original songs from lyrics you wrote (or have the rights to use) and style descriptions. By submitting lyrics you confirm they are yours to use. The service has no catalog of existing songs, no artist voices, and no cover mode.

STYLES, NOT SONGS

Style templates and style descriptions name musical styles — genre, era, mood, tempo, instrumentation, vocal type. They never describe a specific song's melody or a specific artist. Asking the service to imitate a named singer, clone anyone else's voice, or reproduce an existing song is not allowed; prompts and lyrics are screened for artist names and impersonation requests before generation, and a blocked request is never charged.

YOUR VOICE — YOURS ONLY

My Voice clones one voice: yours. Creating a voice requires recording yourself speaking a phrase we issue, plus your explicit confirmation that the voice is your own. Cloning, or attempting to clone, anyone else's voice — a singer, a public figure, a friend, anyone — is prohibited, and a voice found to be someone else's will be removed. Your voice model exists only to sing your songs in this service; it is not sold, shared, or used for anything else. You may delete it at any time in Settings → My Voice; deletion removes the recordings and the model immediately and is not a refund for a voice that was trained successfully.

AI-GENERATED, LABELED

Every song is AI-generated and is labeled as such in the app and in exported files. Never present a song from this service as a human performance.

CREDITS — HOW THE MONEY WORKS, IN WRITING

Credits are prepaid. A Full Song costs 10 credits and a Draft costs 2; the price is shown before every generation. Voice features are priced in credits and shown in the app before you confirm: creating your voice model is charged only when its training succeeds, and converting a song into your voice adds its stated price to that song's cost, charged only when the converted song is delivered. When a generation starts, its credits are held; they are spent only when a song is delivered. If generation fails for any reason — provider error, timeout, wrong output — the hold is released and you are not charged. If a delivered Chinese song mispronounces a word, report it from the player (once per song) and the credits return. Credits never expire. Credit packs are consumable purchases. Weekly, monthly and yearly subscriptions grant their credits at the start of each period; they renew automatically in the App Store or Google Play until cancelled there, and cancelling keeps every credit already granted.

YOUR RIGHTS IN YOUR SONGS

Songs you make while your account has never bought credits are licensed to you for personal use. The moment you buy any credit pack or subscription, every song you have made — including earlier free and trial songs — is licensed to you for full commercial use, retroactively. You are responsible for what you do with a song, including checking that your own lyrics do not infringe anyone's rights.

AVAILABILITY

Generation depends on third-party AI providers. When a provider is unavailable, generation pauses and the app says so; holds are released, so an unavailable period never costs you credits.

DELETING YOUR ACCOUNT

Deleting your account deletes your songs, jobs, ledger and profile, including any unspent credit balance. Store purchase and subscription records remain with Apple/Google under their own policies; deleting the account does not cancel a store subscription — cancel that in the store's subscription settings.
