RealMusic AI — Delete Your Account & Data

Effective: November 15, 2026 · Anni Yang / Venturebnb LLC · Contact: info@annizon.com

Everyone who creates songs with RealMusic AI signs in (an email sign-in link — no password), because your songs and your credit balance belong to your account and must be there on any phone you use. You can delete your account and its server records at any time, right in the app. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Delete your account and its server records (in the app)

  1. Open RealMusic AI and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

This deletes, from our server (provided by Supabase): your account itself, your profile, your songs together with their audio files, your generation jobs and their history, your credit ledger — including any unspent credit balance — and your devices' push-notification tokens. Other people's accounts and songs are never touched by your request. You are signed out when it finishes.

Delete your data on this device

Removing the RealMusic AI app from your phone removes its on-device data (your language preference and sign-in session). That does not delete your account on our server — use Delete my account above for that.

Delete by email

If you no longer have the app installed, email info@annizon.com with the subject “Delete my RealMusic AI account”, from the email address you used to sign in (or tell us that address). We will delete your account and its stored records and confirm by reply.

What is not deleted or cancelled by these steps

Purchase and subscription records held by the Apple App Store, Google Play, or RevenueCat are those providers' records and are not deleted by deleting your RealMusic AI account. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription: if you subscribed (weekly, monthly, or yearly), cancel it in your store's subscription settings to stop future charges. Credits already granted are never taken back while your account exists — but deleting the account deletes its credit balance with it, as the in-app confirmation states.
